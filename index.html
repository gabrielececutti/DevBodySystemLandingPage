The whole system on one page

Eight rules. No guesswork between sessions.

This is the actual summary page from the book. The chapters explain each rule, show the workouts, and cover the edge cases.

01

Train 3× a week

Non-consecutive days. Rotate sessions A → B → C → A.

02

Don't reset

The rotation carries across weeks. Missed C on Friday? Next session is C.

03

Leave 2 reps in the tank

End each set when you could still do about 2 more clean reps.

04

Log every working set

Weight and reps. Warm-ups don't count.

05

Earn the weight increase

Hit the top of the range → add the smallest increment. Otherwise, one more rep.

06

Short on time?

Do the first 4 exercises × 2 sets. Skip exercises 5 and 6.

07

Only 2 visits?

Just follow the rotation. Last time was B? This week is C and A.

08

Never pay back

Missed workouts are gone. Don't double up. Just continue.

The progression rule

Know exactly what to do next time.

The book uses double progression: add reps until you reach the top of the range, then add weight and build the reps back up.

Before you leave the gym, you write one line per exercise. Next session, you just read it and lift. No working it out again.

Crunch weeks

Releases happen. The plan already knows what to do.

Most plans assume a perfect week. This one has a decision table for the weeks that aren't, plus two Fallback sessions built for 2-visit weeks.

Three things never to do

  • Cram two workouts into one visit.
  • Rush a full workout by skipping warm-ups or rest.
  • Add missed sessions on top of next week.

FAQ

Questions before you buy

How much time does it take?

Three gym visits a week of about 50–70 minutes each. If a visit is short, there's a Short session (8 sets). If you only have two slots, there are two Fallback sessions designed for that.

Do I need gym experience?

Basic experience, yes. The plan is built for people with a gym membership who already know their way around the machines. If you haven't learned the Romanian deadlift, the book recommends getting coached first or using the dumbbell version.

What equipment do I need?

A regular commercial gym: leg press, chest press, lat pulldown or pull-up station, cables, dumbbells and a barbell. If a machine is taken, there's a swap table with alternatives.

Does it cover nutrition or cardio?

No. This book covers strength training only. It includes short notes on sleep, meals around training and general cardio guidelines, but it's not a nutrition plan.

What format is it, and how do I get it?

It's a digital ebook sold through Payhip. After checkout you get a download link straight away. The training log is designed to be printed or copied into your notes app.

Will I get results in four weeks?

The first four weeks are about building the habit: booked slots, real starting numbers and a clear next target for every exercise. Results vary between people, and the book doesn't promise a timeline.
